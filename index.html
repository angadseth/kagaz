The difference

Every other free PDF site uploads your file to a server, works on it there, and sends it back. That file was a tax form. A contract. An ID scan.

Kagaz does the work in the tab you already have open.

Not a dig at them — a server costs money, so a free tier has to end somewhere. Kagaz has no server, so there is nothing to meter.

Why it is free

There is no server to pay for.

No limits to hit

No three-a-day cap, no watermark on the way out, no pass to buy on the fourth file. The cost of running a tool is your own laptop, for a second or two, so there is nothing to meter.

Check it yourself

The counter pinned to the bottom of the screen reports two things live: how many bytes have left this page, and how many requests went to any other site. Both stay at zero. It is a measurement, not a promise — open your browser's network tab and compare.

Works with the plug pulled

Load a tool, turn off your Wi‑Fi, and use it anyway. Everything it needs arrived with the page. That is the same reason your file has nowhere to go.
